Blood Donation Lifeline

Active Development

A centralized, real-time emergency blood donation web application engineered to bridge the critical time gap during medical crises. Connects urgent blood seekers with voluntary registered donors nearby based on geographic proximity and Rh compatibility.

  • Blood Compatibility Matcher: Immediate verification of donor-recipient Rh factor compatibility matrix.
